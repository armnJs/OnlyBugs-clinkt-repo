### 2. 📊 FINDINGS REPORT (PDF/Slides) — **15 pts Core + 10 pts Innovation**
This is the **biggest scoring item**. The judges want:

**Structure (start with your conclusion!):**
1. **What happened** — The core issue (e.g., "Clinkt is losing ₹84,857 in revenue from cart abandonment — 70% of carts are abandoned, driven by chronic stock-outs and the Family segment")
2. **Evidence chain** — Connect the dots:
   - 60.7% of product-days are in sub-optimal stock
   - Family segment has 79.5% abandonment (worst)
   - Instant Noodles & Bath Soap are critically understocked
   - Abandoned revenue (₹84.8K) > Ordered revenue (₹48.4K)
3. **What you ruled out** — Show you tested alternatives (e.g., "Is it a price issue? No — avg abandoned price ₹127 ≈ avg ordered price ₹124")
4. **Business impact** — Quantified: ₹84,857 lost, 666 abandoned carts, 3 products chronically out of stock
5. **Recommendation** — What Clinkt should DO about it
