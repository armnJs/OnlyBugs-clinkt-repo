Clinkt Case Investigation — Findings Report

Where Clinkt's Revenue Goes to Die

An evidence-driven investigation into why customers arrive with intent, add products to their carts, and then vanish — and what Clinkt must fix to survive.

Our Verdict: Clinkt is losing ₹84,857 in abandoned cart revenue — 1.75× its actual earned revenue — due to three interconnected failures: a broken recommendation engine that shows irrelevant products, chronic inventory mismanagement that leaves high-demand items understocked, and a fractured customer journey where 73.1% of sessions end without a purchase. These are not three separate problems. They are one failure loop that feeds itself.

Finding 04 — Evidence Chain

The Recommendation Engine Is Broken

Products being surfaced to customers don't match what they actually want to buy. The View-to-Order gap reveals what's being shown vs what converts.

Observation
Home Care receives 15.5% of all views but only 9.0% of orders — a 6.5 percentage point gap. Meanwhile, Staples & Grains gets only 8.3% of views but 11.5% of orders.
📁 exports/investigation_3_recommendation_gap.xlsx
Worst Converting Products Get Most Views
Laundry Detergent — 209 views but only 21.5% view-to-cart rate. Tomatoes — 78 views, 20.5% conversion. These are being pushed by the platform but customers aren't buying them.

Meanwhile, products like Toor Dal 1 kg convert at high rates but get fewer views — they're being hidden.
📁 exports/investigation_1_product_funnel.xlsx → View_to_Cart_Rate
Session Bounce Evidence
264 sessions (32.6%) are view-only — the customer browses and leaves without adding anything to cart. Average duration: just 1.4 minutes.

Compare this to sessions that order: 9.4 minutes average. Customers who find relevant products engage 6.7× longer.
📁 exports/investigation_4_session_journeys.xlsx
Ruled Out: Category Mismatch
In sessions that DO result in orders, 100% of ordered categories were previously viewed. This means when the right product IS shown, customers DO buy. The problem is that too many sessions never show the right product.
📁 full_investigation.py → Category_Match_Pct analysis
