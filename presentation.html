Scientific Rigor

Competing Hypotheses Tested & Ruled Out

We validated our root causes by disproving alternative explanations.

1. Price Sensitivity?

RULED OUT

Abandoned items average ₹127.41 vs ordered items at ₹123.63 (only 3.0% difference). Price elasticity is not the root cause.

2. Total Supply Depletion?

RULED OUT

Slow movers like Cookies and Cola maintained healthy stock for 31 of 31 days. This is a prioritization failure, not a warehouse shortage.

3. Accidental Traffic?

RULED OUT

In purchasing sessions, 100% of ordered categories were viewed first. High intent converts when relevant stock exists.
